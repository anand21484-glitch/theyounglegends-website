Classroom uses

Four ways in, one approach.

Each starts with a person or a question, and ends with students doing something themselves.

History

Meet the people behind the chapter

Open a lesson with an Azadi Tales story, then walk the Freedom Timeline as a class.

Science

Predict before you demonstrate

Run a Curious Minds experiment with a whole-class prediction poll first.

Values

Discuss real choices

Use a hero’s dilemma to talk about courage, kindness and leadership.

Clubs

Run a curiosity club

Weekly mini challenges students tackle in small teams.

What a session could look like

Forty minutes, four moves.

A simple structure teachers can adapt to any period length.

01

Story

Open with a hero or a question on the big screen.

~10 min
02

Discuss

Students predict, argue and explain their thinking.

~10 min
03

Do

A hands-on experiment, puzzle or challenge in groups.

~15 min
04

Reflect

What did we learn? What do we want to find out next?

~5 min
