Our story

Every child is curious. Every curious mind can become a legend.

The Young Legends exists to help children discover the extraordinary people, ideas and discoveries that shaped India and the world — and to imagine who they might become.

Why we exist

We don’t want children to just know more. We want them to become more curious — to ask why, look for evidence, and believe they can do remarkable things too.

Questions over answers

A question that genuinely bothers a child stays with them for years. A fact learned for a test leaves by the weekend.

People over chapters

Children remember people, stakes and choices — so every topic begins with someone real.

Doing over memorising

The best way to understand something is to try it yourself, with your own hands.
