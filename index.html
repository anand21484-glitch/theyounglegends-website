How curiosity becomes learning

Every lesson starts with a question.

One experiment, start to finish — the way children meet it in Curious Minds. Scroll to follow along.

See → Predict → Do → Observe → Understand → Explore

The floating egg
+ 6 SPOONS SALT
Fresh water 1.00Egg 1.03Salt water 1.10
01 · See

An egg sinks to the bottom of a glass of water.

  1. 01

    See.

    It starts with something ordinary. An egg, a glass of water. It sinks — of course it does. Or does it have to?

  2. 02

    Predict.

    Before touching anything, the child commits to a guess. A prediction is the first step of thinking like a scientist.

  3. 03

    Do.

    Real materials from the kitchen. Clear steps, a parent nearby, nothing that needs a lab.

  4. 04

    Observe.

    Watch carefully. Was the prediction right? What exactly changed — and when?

  5. 05

    Understand.

    Now the why: density, explained with the experiment still in front of them — not a definition to memorise.

  6. 06

    Explore.

    Every answer opens a new question — and the next challenge is waiting.

For parents & educators

Thoughtful learning, not just screen time.

We build around how children actually learn: through people they can relate to, questions they want answered and things they can do with their own hands.

What children experience

Short illustrated stories, predictions before experiments, quizzes that reward reasoning, and badges for curiosity — not speed.

Learning value

History becomes memorable through people; science becomes understandable through doing. Children practise asking, predicting and checking evidence.

Safety & responsible use

Age-appropriate content, no unnecessary distractions, progress you can see, and offline learning in both apps.

What ages are the apps designed for?

Our apps are designed for children aged roughly 5 to 15. Younger children enjoy them most alongside a parent; older children can explore on their own.

Do the apps work offline?

Yes. Azadi Tales and Curious Minds both work 100% offline — no internet needed, so learning can travel with you.

Are the XP points and badges a test of intelligence?

No. XP and badges reward effort and progress — finishing stories, quizzes and experiments — not ability.

Where can I download the apps?

Azadi Tales and Curious Minds are available on Google Play. Links are on each app’s page.
